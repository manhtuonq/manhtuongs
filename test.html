The Process
01
Source

Every collage begins with accumulation — magazines, newspapers, maps, photographs, found ephemera collected over months or years.

02
Tear

Scissors are secondary. The hand reveals edges that blades cannot — unpredictable, fibrous, alive. Tearing is a form of drawing.

03
Compose

Arrangement without adhesive first. Elements shift, rotate, overlap. Negative space earns equal weight to the fragments placed within it.

04
Commit

Paste is irreversible. The final act of fixing transforms intention into artifact — a record of a single moment of decision.
